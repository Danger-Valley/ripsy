RIPSY turns rock, paper, scissors into a hidden-information strategy battle.

Create a private room, invite an opponent, arrange your lineup, and outthink them on a tactical board. Every move is synchronized through the RIPSY game server, so both players always recover the authoritative game state after reconnecting.

Features:
• Private multiplayer rooms
• Hidden lineups and tactical movement
• Animated battles
• Invitation links and room codes
• Turn and game notifications
• Automatic reconnect recovery

No wallet, cryptocurrency, or blockchain is required.
