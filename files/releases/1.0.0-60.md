# RIPSY 1.0.0 (60)

Release date: 2026-10-12. Source baseline: `ad79113` plus synchronized native build number 60 and a generated Watchman-cookie ignore rule.

## Included changes

- Your Army replaces its wooden tray with the same light collection card, compact two-by-seven numbered slots and cyan/navy selection. Reset, Save, one collectible per slot, free fallback and inventory behavior are unchanged. Portrait heads and the last slot's rounded edge fit fully.
- Native Shop has no View Purchase/history buttons. Terminal saved receipts no longer disable new Buy actions; unfinished requests keep Continue purchase, durable recovery and duplicate-payment guards. New purchases verify the old final status before replacing a receipt.
- Seeker uses only its external wallet across Home/Shop/Army, without email registration, account switching or email-session errors. iPhone and ordinary Android retain email accounts. Historical orders are preserved and never reassigned or automatically re-signed.
- Illustrated splash uses aspect fill with explicit measured-root bounds on both platforms, correcting the intrinsic-image-size overflow while keeping safe-area logo/loading overlays.
- Retains direct Buy/SOL signing, remote figure catalog, prices/odds, purchase authorization, gameplay/Rive timing and the existing update policy. No backend/onchain mutation.
