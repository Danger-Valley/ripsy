# Brand — RIPSY

RIPSY is a tactical multiplayer game where hidden rock-paper-scissors armies battle to capture the flag, with optional collectible figures.

## Palette — Toy Box

**Vibe:** bright · tactile · friendly · adventurous
**Category:** memecoin/playful
**Mood:** playful · bold

Toy Box presents RIPSY as a physical tabletop world: clear sky-blue canvases, warm cream play surfaces, sand-colored boards and chunky molded-plastic controls. Coral identifies rivals and destructive moments, cyan identifies the player and informational actions, while reward yellow carries the primary play action. The direction is original and does not copy characters, maps, assets or trade dress from another game.

## Typography — Space Grotesk + Space Mono

- **Display and body:** Space Grotesk
- **Mono:** Space Mono for timers, room codes, wallet addresses and numbers that update in place

Web fonts are self-hosted through `next/font/local` in `frontend/apps/web/app/layout.tsx`, using `--font-sans` and `--font-mono`; production builds do not depend on Google Fonts network access. Native builds should bundle matching static font files before replacing the system fallback.

The illustrated native Toy Box surfaces currently use the shared `homeFont`
fallback (`ArialRoundedMTBold` on iOS, `sans-serif-medium` on Android) for their
rounded game headings/actions. Addresses remain native monospace and clocks use
tabular digits. Keep these choices consistent through secondary dialogs until a
matching rounded font is bundled for both platforms; do not silently claim native
Space Grotesk/Space Mono assets are installed.

## Gradients

Gradients are optional, never required. Native creates depth with stacked flat colors and generated scene art. Web may use restrained sky, cream or sand gradients for large scenery surfaces, but controls retain solid semantic colors and darker bottom edges.

## Shape, depth and motion

- Primary controls use 18–24 px radii, a light inset highlight, a darker 4–7 px bottom edge and a short press translation.
- Cards use 24–36 px radii, a bright top edge, a dark bottom edge and one soft shadow. Avoid nested bordered rectangles.
- Touch targets are at least 44×44 px.
- Micro-interactions run for 120–180 ms; screen entrances for 240–320 ms.
- Motion uses transform and opacity only and respects Reduce Motion.
- The battle board uses a warm sand checkerboard with a molded yellow frame. Decorative scenery must stay outside cells and never change board hit areas.
- Native Home and Battle now use original generated Toy Box backdrops from `frontend/apps/mobile/src/assets/ui`. Home mascots are decorative artwork, not new playable/NFT variants. The current `battle-toy-frame-v2.png` has a centered **84%** playable square (`BOARD_PLAYABLE_RATIO = 0.84`); native checkerboard tiles, piece, touch, movement, combat and trap coordinates share that inset. Only the decorative frame image is clipped, never the figure layer.
- Secondary UI uses `ToyDialog`, `ToySectionHeading`, `ToyBadge` and `ToyHomeButton`: cream scrollable safe-area cards, existing menu/weapon sprites, compact rounded headings, whole-card choices and explicit close/return actions. Auth remains keyboard-safe; required tie choices cannot dismiss. Busy buttons keep their labels and lock double submission. Status cards have uniform rounded silhouettes, not uneven top/bottom outlines. Focus is painted above the button face.
- No extra entrance motion was added to these secondary dialogs. Preserve existing gameplay Rive timing and reduced-motion-aware lineup attention; do not restart combat while opening a picker.
- Your Army's lineup now uses the same light collection card instead of a wooden tray. Neutral numbered slots use the shared muted collection surface; cyan and a navy outline mark selection. Keep the two rows of seven and existing portraits, without nested label panels or ornamental slot shadows.

## Tone and voice

### Use

Short, self-aware and action-first copy. Prefer `Play now`, `Your move`, `Army ready`, `Nice hit` and specific jokes tied to the game.

### Avoid

Corporate explanations, generic crypto hype, forced memes, long button labels and jokes that need explaining.

### Example

> Your army is ready. Go steal that flag.

## Usage

**Do:**

- Reference semantic tokens from a shared theme module.
- Use reward yellow for special actions, prizes, counters and victory emphasis.
- Keep hot pink focused on primary actions and selection.
- Use cyan for secondary characters, collectible emphasis and informational highlights.
- Use Space Mono with tabular figures for timers and changing values.
- Provide loading, empty, error, success and disabled states.

**Don't:**

- Hardcode screen-level colors.
- Decorate every surface with gradients.
- Use glow as a permanent outline around every card.
- Place decorative elements over board cells or figures.
- Copy Candy Crush characters, map, candy assets or trade dress.
